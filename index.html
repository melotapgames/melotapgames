About MeloTap Games

We are building a brand centered around casual mobile entertainment, with a focus on accessible design, quality gameplay feel, and products that can grow over time.

MeloTap Games is the game brand of Shanghai Lingqu Fangcheng Technology Co., Ltd.

Our company focuses on the development of casual and puzzle-oriented mobile games. We are interested in creating colorful, polished, and approachable experiences that deliver clear mechanics, satisfying feedback, and strong replay value.

Rather than chasing short-term novelty alone, we care about building mobile games that feel good to play, are easy to understand, and can remain enjoyable for players over time.
