What we believe

Casual games can be easy to enter without feeling ordinary. We care about the rhythm, color, clarity, and craft behind every tap.

Creative direction

Familiar play, seen with fresh eyes.

We explore accessible puzzle formats and look for the detail that makes each interaction clearer, warmer, and more memorable. These are development interests—not announced products.

01 / MATCH

Match & combine

Match-3, tile matching, pair matching, and mahjong-inspired ideas built around recognition and satisfying resolution.

02 / SOLVE

Clear & connect

Block and connection puzzles that turn a small set of readable rules into thoughtful challenges.

03 / FLOW

Move & find flow

Directional and arrow-based concepts shaped through clean motion, pace, and tactile feedback.

Inside the name

Melody meets tap.

“MeloTap” brings rhythm together with the simplest mobile action. The name expresses our ambition for play that feels intuitive, upbeat, and quietly memorable.

Melo · rhythm    Tap · interaction
How we think

A clear idea is only the beginning.

The feeling of a game lives in the details around its rules. These principles guide the foundation we are building.

01

Clear at first touch

Players should understand the invitation quickly, with room for discovery to unfold.

02

Made to feel good

Motion, feedback, color, and pacing should work together with purpose.

03

Ready for the world

Accessible ideas and a visual language that can travel naturally across cultures.
