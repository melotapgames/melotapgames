Privacy Policy

How MeloTap Games handles information in our mobile games, applications, websites, and related services.

Effective August 26, 2026
Policy scopeMeloTap Games services
WorldwidePrivacy contact ↗

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games, applications, websites, and related services provided under the MeloTap Games brand that link to this policy. Not every Service uses every feature or collects every category of information described below. The data practices of a particular Service depend on its platform, version, features, permissions, and third-party services.

1. Introduction

Shanghai Lingqu Fangcheng Technology Co., Ltd. (上海灵趣方程科技有限公司), operating under the brand name MeloTap Games (collectively, “MeloTap Games,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we and our service providers may collect, how it is used and disclosed, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using a Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Scope

This policy applies when you:

  • download, access, or play a MeloTap Games mobile game or application that links to this policy;
  • use online features such as cloud saves, leaderboards, achievements, in-app purchases, advertising, or push notifications when they are available;
  • visit melotapgames.com or another website operated by us that links to this policy; or
  • contact us regarding a Service or a privacy request.

Core gameplay in some Services may be available offline. Advertising, analytics, cloud saves, leaderboards, purchases, notifications, attribution, updates, and other online features require an internet connection and may involve data transmission.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on the Service and how you use it, we or our service providers may collect the following categories of information.

3.1 Gameplay and Service Data

  • game progress, completed levels, scores, achievements, virtual items, settings, and feature usage;
  • cloud-save records, leaderboard entries, and an internal or platform-generated player identifier where those features are available;
  • session dates, session duration, interactions, and in-game events; and
  • push notification token and notification interaction data if notifications are enabled.

3.2 Device, Technical, and Diagnostic Data

  • device model, device type, operating system and version, language, time zone, country or region, carrier, and network type;
  • app version, package or bundle identifier, installation information, and service configuration;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP address or device settings;
  • device and app identifiers, which may include Android ID, IDFV, installation IDs, and similar identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers, such as GAID or IDFA, where available and permitted by your device settings and applicable law; and
  • crash logs, error reports, diagnostics, performance information, and technical events.

3.3 Advertising, Analytics, and Attribution Data

  • advertisement requests, impressions, views, clicks, rewards, and interaction events;
  • app installs, re-installs, opens, campaign source, conversion events, and attribution information;
  • consent status and privacy preference signals; and
  • aggregated or pseudonymous analytics about how a Service is used.

3.4 Purchase Information

If you make an in-app purchase, we may receive an order or transaction identifier, product SKU, purchase time, purchase status, receipt validation result, and related transaction information from Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details; those are processed by the applicable app store or payment provider.

3.5 Location Information

A Service may request access to precise or approximate device location only if a location-based feature is offered. Location permission is requested through your device and is accessed only after you grant permission. You can withdraw permission through your device settings. If a particular Service does not request location permission, we do not collect precise device location through that Service. We may still infer a general country or region from an IP address for security, localization, analytics, or advertising.

3.6 Information You Provide

If you contact us, we receive the email address and any game name, player or device identifier, message, attachment, or other information you choose to provide. Our current Services do not require a MeloTap Games account and do not intentionally collect a user's name, phone number, address, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera content through normal gameplay.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • provide, operate, maintain, and improve our Services;
  • save and synchronize progress, operate leaderboards and achievements, and provide requested features;
  • process and validate in-app purchases and restore purchases;
  • deliver push notifications where enabled;
  • measure performance, understand usage, conduct analytics, and develop new features;
  • diagnose crashes, correct errors, and improve stability;
  • display, measure, personalize, and limit advertising in accordance with your choices and applicable law;
  • measure advertising campaigns, installations, and attribution;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our terms;
  • respond to inquiries and privacy requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights, users, and Services.

5. Advertising and Your Choices

Some Services contain advertising. Subject to your consent, device settings, platform requirements, and applicable law, advertising may be personalized using advertising identifiers, approximate location, app activity, ad interactions, and information received by advertising partners. If personalized advertising is unavailable or you decline consent, you may still receive contextual or non-personalized advertising.

On iOS, a Service will request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the IDFA or tracking you across apps and websites when such permission is required. On Android and iOS, you can limit advertising tracking, reset or delete an advertising identifier, and manage permissions through your device settings. Where a Service provides a Privacy or Consent Settings menu, you can also update advertising choices there.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, personalized advertising and the disclosure of identifiers and usage information to advertising partners may be considered “selling,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some U.S. state privacy laws. Where applicable, you may opt out using the Service's privacy settings, your device privacy controls, or by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We may disclose information to service providers and partners that help us operate, analyze, monetize, secure, distribute, and support our Services. Depending on the particular Service, these may include:

  • Google, including AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play services, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Cloud Messaging;
  • AppLovin, including AppLovin MAX and advertising partners made available through its mediation platform;
  • Unity, including Unity Ads and ironSource LevelPlay and advertising partners made available through those platforms;
  • AppsFlyer for mobile measurement, attribution, analytics, and fraud prevention;
  • Apple, including the App Store, in-app purchase services, Game Center, push-notification services, and platform privacy services where applicable; and
  • cloud hosting, content delivery, security, professional advisers, and other technical providers necessary to operate a Service.

Advertising mediation platforms may select from multiple advertising networks. The specific providers active in a Service can vary by platform, region, version, consent status, and configuration. These third parties may process information under their own privacy policies and, in some cases, act as independent controllers of that information.

For more information, please review the relevant provider policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information when required by law or valid legal process; to protect rights, safety, property, and security; to investigate fraud or abuse; with your direction or consent; or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or transfer of assets. Any successor will be required to handle information consistently with this policy or provide appropriate notice.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the laws of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction require a legal basis, we process information as applicable:

  • to perform a contract with you or provide a Service you request;
  • with your consent, including for personalized advertising, tracking, push notifications, or device permissions where consent is required;
  • for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, analyzing, and improving the Services, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

You may withdraw consent at any time through available in-app or device controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

8. International Data Transfers

We are based in China and make our Services available worldwide. Information may be processed in China, the United States, Singapore, the European Economic Area, or other countries where we or our service providers operate. These countries may have data-protection laws different from those in your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as contractual protections, adequacy mechanisms, or consent.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the Service, maintaining cloud progress, analyzing and improving performance, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal, tax, accounting, or app-store obligations.

Retention periods vary according to the data type, the Service, your choices, and provider configuration. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies and contractual obligations.

10. Data Deletion

Our current Services do not require a MeloTap Games account. You can delete data stored locally on your device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may not delete information already transmitted to us or to a service provider.

To request deletion of data associated with a Service, email melotapgames@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request.” Please include the name of the game, your platform, and any in-game player ID, installation ID, or other identifier shown in the game's settings. Do not send government identification documents unless we specifically request information reasonably necessary to verify the request.

We will process verified requests within the period required by applicable law. We may retain limited information when legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance. Where applicable, we will also instruct service providers acting on our behalf to delete the relevant information.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include access controls, encryption in transit where supported, monitoring, and limiting access to personnel and providers with a business need. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required to consent to personal-data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please email melotapgames@gmail.com with the subject “Child Privacy Request.” We will take reasonable steps to investigate and delete the information where required.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access to or a copy of your personal information;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent;
  • request data portability;
  • opt out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information as defined by applicable law;
  • appeal a decision concerning a privacy request where applicable; and
  • submit a complaint to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, email melotapgames@gmail.com and describe your request. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and locate relevant data. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

14. Website Data

When you visit melotapgames.com, our hosting, content-delivery, and security providers may process IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, referring page, and similar network or log information needed to deliver and protect the website. The website is delivered through Cloudflare. You can review the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

At the effective date above, we do not intentionally use first-party behavioral analytics or advertising cookies on the company website. If that changes materially, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choices required by law.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our Services, data practices, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice in the relevant Service or obtain consent before a material change takes effect.
