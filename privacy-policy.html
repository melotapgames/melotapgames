This policy applies to this company website. Any future MeloTap Games mobile game or service may have a separate, product-specific privacy policy describing its actual data practices.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Shanghai Lingqu Fangcheng Technology Co., Ltd. (上海灵趣方程科技有限公司), using the brand name MeloTap Games. In this policy, “MeloTap Games,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to this company.

2. Information we receive

This website does not currently provide a contact form or public email channel. Our hosting, content delivery, and security providers may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, referring page, and similar network or log information needed to deliver and protect the website.

If a direct contact channel is added later, we will update this policy to explain how correspondence is handled.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website; respond to inquiries; communicate about potential business relationships; detect and prevent misuse; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and analytics

At the effective date shown above, we do not intentionally use advertising cookies or first-party behavioral analytics on this website. Infrastructure providers may use strictly necessary technologies or process network data to provide security, content delivery, and reliability. If our use of cookies or analytics changes materially, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choices required by applicable law.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We may use service providers for website hosting, content delivery, security, and email. These providers process information on our behalf or under their own applicable terms. Our website is delivered through Cloudflare; you can read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request; to protect rights, safety, and security; or in connection with a corporate transaction. We do not sell personal information collected through this website.

6. Retention and security

We retain correspondence and related information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including maintaining business records and meeting legal obligations. Technical logs are generally retained according to our service providers’ settings and policies.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. International processing

Because this website is available globally and uses internet infrastructure providers, information may be processed in countries or regions outside your place of residence. Where required, we take appropriate steps to handle such processing in accordance with applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, or restriction, or objecting to certain processing. You may contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where permitted or required by law.

9. Children

This company website is not directed to children and is not designed to collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this website, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as our website, services, or legal obligations evolve. We will post the updated version here and change the effective date when we make material updates.

11. Contact

Official contact details will be published on our Contact page after activation.
