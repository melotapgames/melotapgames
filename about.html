Our story

A studio shaped around the joy of simple interaction.

MeloTap Games is the game brand of Shanghai Lingqu Fangcheng Technology Co., Ltd., based in Shanghai, China.

Our focus is casual and puzzle-led mobile games: experiences that make sense quickly, reward attention, and bring a little brightness to the spaces between everything else.

We are at the beginning of a long journey. Rather than presenting concepts as finished products, we are building the studio, craft, and brand foundation that future releases can grow from.

How we work

Three principles keep us moving.

They guide how we evaluate an idea, shape the player experience, and make decisions as a growing studio.

01

Find the clear idea

We begin with a simple, legible interaction and look for the twist that gives it character and staying power.

02

Craft the feeling

We refine the moments around the mechanic—feedback, pacing, motion, sound, and the details players may feel before they notice.

03

Grow with intention

We build for lasting quality, learning from every iteration and giving each product the time to find its strongest form.
