About MeloTap Games

A growing game brand focused on casual mobile experiences, polished design, and long-term global development.

MeloTap Games is the game brand of Shanghai Lingqu Fangcheng Technology Co., Ltd.

We focus on casual mobile game development with an emphasis on enjoyable interaction, approachable design, and a player-friendly experience. Our interests include puzzle gameplay, matching mechanics, and other light, accessible mobile game forms that can appeal to a broad global audience.

Our vision is to create game experiences that are simple to begin, satisfying to play, and durable enough to grow into long-term products and recognizable brand assets.

Our Direction

We are currently focused on developing our brand foundation and exploring the most promising casual game directions.
